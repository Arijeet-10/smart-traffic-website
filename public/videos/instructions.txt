BENGALFLOW AI VIDEO ASSETS
==========================

Place your junction-specific YOLO detection videos (MP4 format) in this folder.
The application dashboard will fetch these videos based on the paths defined in 'src/app/lib/cities-data.ts'.

Expected File Names Examples:
- kolkata_park_circus.mp4
- howrah_station.mp4
- darjeeling_chowrasta.mp4

If a video is missing, the dashboard will display the native HTML5 video 'not found' state.
